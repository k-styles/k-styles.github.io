01 · About

Hi — I'm Kartik. I like cats, pizzas, and thinking hard about the computational nature of quantum many-body systems. I'm a Master's student in Computer Science at TUHH, in Prof. Martin Kliesch's group at the Institute for Quantum Inspired & Quantum Optimization.

Before quantum, I did deep-learning research at IIT Delhi's Vision Lab — a paper at ACCV 2024. In 2024 my interests shifted decisively toward quantum complexity: Quantum Hamiltonian Complexity, the quantum PCP conjecture and its hardness-of-approximation formulation. I've also collaborated with the Quantum Information Theory Group at Seoul National University.
